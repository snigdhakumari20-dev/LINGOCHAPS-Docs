Trim → Transcribe Workflow Guide

Any source file (like MP4, MP3, AMR, or other video and audio formats) starts in the Video Trimmer desktop tool. Cut it down to only the parts you need before uploading it to the transcription tool. Smaller files upload faster, transcribe quicker, and are much easier to work with!

Core Mechanics

The process starts with local asset trimming via the Video Trimmer desktop utility, followed by automated ingestion and processing in the Lingochaps Transcription Engine, and finishes with quality checking generated dialogues and Word reports.

Stage 01: Video Trimmer (Local Preparation)

Start by trimming your video or audio file on your computer using the Video Trimmer tool.

1

Open the source file

Click the Open Video button and choose your file from your computer. We support both video and audio files. Toggle the Video / Audio only switch at the top to match your file type.

Formats: MP4, MKV, MOV, MP3, M4A, AMR, WAV, etc.
2

Mark the range you need

Drag the slider to find where your clip starts and ends. Click Set Start and Set End. Then click Add to List to put it in the queue. You can repeat this to cut multiple clips from the same file.

3

Or use Auto-split instead

If you want to split the file into equal parts, use the Auto-split section. Type a number in Into ___ parts and click Generate. Or set a time length under Every ___ h ___ m ___ s and click Generate. This is the fastest way to break a long recording into smaller pieces.

  • Splitting a 30-minute file Into 2 parts yields:
    • Segment 1: 00:00:00 → 00:15:00 [queued]
    • Segment 2: 00:15:00 → 00:30:00 [queued]
  • Exported files show a [trimmed] suffix upon write completion.
  • The status footer confirms "All segments trimmed!" when the process completes.
4

Pick a save folder

Click Choose... next to Save folder to pick where you want to save the trimmed files. It defaults to a folder named trimmed video in your Downloads folder.

5

Convert to MP3 if you only need audio

Tick the Convert to MP3 box before exporting if you don't need the video. This keeps the file size small for the next stage.

Recommended for call recordings like AMR/MP3 files
6

Export

Click Trim All to render all the clips in your list. Then click Download or Open Folder to get your finished files. These are the files you will upload in Stage 02.

Stage 02: Upload & Transcribe

Once your trimmed file is ready on your computer, open the Lingochaps tool → Transcription workspace. This is an internal tool, so always sign in with your official @lingochaps email.

1

Sign in & open New transcription

Log in with your official Lingochaps ID. Click the Transcription card on the left menu (it is the main active module) to open a new job.

2

Step 1 — upload the trimmed file

Click the upload box or drag your file in. The system accepts video (MP4, MKV, MOV...) and audio (MP3, M4A, WAV, AMR...) — use the exact files you exported from the trimmer.

3

Step 2 — set languages & instructions

Leave the Languages field on auto-detect unless you know the language being spoken (you can type hints separated by commas if you do). Add any notes for the AI model in Custom instructions (optional).

4

Choose what output you need

Select the option that matches what your project needs:

  • Full — transcription + on-screen text: Extracts speech transcripts, translation maps, proper-noun notes, and any visible on-screen text using optical character recognition (OCR) accompanied by video snapshots.
  • Transcript only: Performs speech recognition, transcription, and translation. Visual processing is omitted, ensuring faster queue processing.
  • On-screen text only: Disables audio transcription entirely. Extracts text overlays and slides found within the video stream.

Fallback server-side trimming

You can check Process only part of the video to isolate bounds server-side. However, this should only be treated as a backup option; manual local trimming is the preferred procedure.

5

Step 3 — start transcription

Click Start transcription. This button only becomes active after you select a file. If you cannot click it, check Step 1 again.

6

Track progress in Recent jobs

All uploaded jobs appear in the Recent jobs list below (newest first). You can see the status badge and progress percentage.

  • DONE / 100%: Ingestion and post-processing completed. Ready for quality review.
  • ERROR: The pipeline failed. Re-trim or re-encode your input file and upload again.
  • Refresh: Click the refresh icon at the top right to poll the latest queue metrics.
  • Delete: Click the trash bin icon to clear history logs. This action deletes all remote storage items permanently.

Stage 03: Review & Download Outputs

Open your finished job to check the translated text or download the finalized Word report.

1

Open the job details

Click on a DONE row from your Recent jobs. The page shows the current progress: Queued → Transcribing → Extracting on-screen text → Generating report → Publishing outputs.

2

Download the Word report

Once the job shows COMPLETE / 100%, click the Download Word report button. The report will save directly to your computer. It contains the dialogue table, translation columns, and notes (plus screenshots if you selected the 'Full' option).

3

Or read it on-screen, minute by minute

You can read the transcription on the page using the minute-by-minute accordion. Click on any minute (like `Minute 1`, `Minute 2`) to expand the segments inside.

4

Read a segment

Each segment shows the speaker, language, and timestamp. The transcription is on the left and the translation is on the right. Code-switched words are highlighted, and proper nouns have a short explanation footnote underneath.

  • Format: Female Speaker 1 [Hindi] 00:07:09–00:07:15
  • Transcription (Left): Multilingual or code-switched dialogues with color-coded token highlights.
  • Translation (Right): Human-readable translated English context.
  • Explanatory note: A footnote explanation for specialized terms or regional contexts (e.g., explaining who a named individual is in the conversation).
